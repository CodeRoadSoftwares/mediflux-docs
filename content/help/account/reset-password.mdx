---
title: How do I reset my password?
description: Recover access if you forgot your password or need to set a new one.
tags: [account, login, password]
related:
  - /docs/introduction/getting-started
---

Use **Forgot password** on the login page. MediFlux emails a reset link to your registered address.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    On the login page, select **Forgot password**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Enter the email address you use to sign in.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Open the email and follow the link to choose a new password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warning" title="No email?">
  Check spam, then confirm the email is the one on the account. If you still cannot reset, use the contact options at the bottom of this page.
</Callout>
