---
title: How do I change my store details?
description: Update your store information from Account settings or contact the MediFlux Support Team for restricted changes.
tags: [account, store, settings]
related:
  - /docs/account/settings
---

Go to **Settings → Account** to view and update your store details.

You can update details such as the **Owner Name**, **Phone**, **Email**, and **Address** directly from the account settings.

The **Store Name** and **License Number** cannot be changed directly. To update either of these details, contact the **MediFlux Support Team**.
