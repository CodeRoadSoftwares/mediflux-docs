---
title: What do I do if I cannot log in?
description: Troubleshoot common login issues and regain access to your MediFlux account.
tags: [account, login, troubleshooting]
related:
  - /docs/account/login
---

If you cannot log in to MediFlux, first check that the **Email** and **Password** you entered are correct. Make sure there are no extra spaces in the password, as even a small difference can prevent you from logging in.

If you are still unable to log in with your password, try logging in using **OTP** instead.

If you still cannot access your account, contact the **MediFlux Support Team** for assistance.
