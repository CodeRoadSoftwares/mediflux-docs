---
title: How do I add another user?
description: Add a new user to your MediFlux account and assign their permissions.
tags: [account, users, user-management]
related:
  - /docs/account/user-management
---

Go to **Settings → User Management** and select **Create user**.

<Steps>

<Step title="Enter user details">

Enter the user's **Role**, **Name**, **Email Address**, and **Phone**.

Under **Security**, enter a **Password** and **Confirm Password**.

</Step>

<Step title="Set permissions">

Under **Permissions**, select the required **Read**, **Write**, and **Delete** permissions for each module.

</Step>

<Step title="Create the user">

Review the details and select **Create User**.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Callout type="note">
The permissions assigned to a user determine what they can view, add, or delete in MediFlux.
</Callout>
