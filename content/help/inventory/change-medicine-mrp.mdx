Change a medicine's **MRP** from **Drug List → Separated View**. You can edit one medicine directly or update several medicines together.

---

## Change the MRP of one medicine

1. Go to **Drug List → Separated View**.
2. Find the medicine, open its **actions (⋮)** menu, and select **Edit**.
3. Update the **MRP** field and save.

You can also select the medicine's checkbox and click **Update** in the toolbar.

---

## Change the MRP of multiple medicines

1. Go to **Drug List → Separated View**.
2. Select the checkboxes for the medicines you want to update.
3. Click **Update** in the toolbar.
4. In the **Bulk Update** window, enter the new value in the **MRP** field.
5. Leave all other fields blank to keep their existing values.
6. Click **Update Drugs**.

Only fields you fill in are changed; blank fields keep their existing values. The same window can also update **Batch**, **HSN**, **Stock**, **Expiry**, **Rack**, and tax fields for all selected medicines.
