title: How do I edit a sale?
description: Update an existing sale from the Sales List in MediFlux.
tags: [sales, sales-list, billing]
related:

---

You can update an existing sale from **Sales → Sales List**.

<Steps>

<Step title="Find the sale">

Search for the sale you want to update using the search bar or available filters.

</Step>

<Step title="Open the update option">

Select the three-dot menu (⋮) on the sale row and choose **Update**.

</Step>

<Step title="Update the sale">

Make the required changes to the sale details and save the updates.

</Step>

</Steps>
