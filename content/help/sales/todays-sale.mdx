---
title: How do I check today's sales?
description: View recent sales and filter the Sales List to check today's sales.
tags: [sales, sales-list, reports]
related:
  - /docs/sales/sale-list
---

You can check today's sales directly from **Sales → Sales List**.

Recent sales are displayed by default, so you can immediately view the latest recorded sales without applying any filters.

To view only today's sales, select **Filter** and set the date to today's date. The Sales List will then display the sales recorded for the selected date.

You can review the sale details, customer information, invoice numbers, and payment details from the list.
