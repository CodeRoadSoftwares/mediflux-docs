---
title: How do I find a previous sale?
description: Find previous sales using the Sales List search and filters.
tags: [sales, sales-list, search]
related:
  - /docs/sales/sale-list
---

Go to **Sales → Sales List** to find a previous sale.

You can search for a sale using the search bar. Enter a **Customer Name**, **Drug Name**, or **Invoice Number** to find the relevant sale.

You can also use the available filters to narrow down the results by sale type or payment method.
