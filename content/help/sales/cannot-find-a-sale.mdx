---
title: How do I find an old sale bill?
description: Search the sales list by bill number, customer, or date range.
tags: [sales, bills, search]
related:
  - /docs/sales/sale-list
---

Open **Sales → Sales List** and use the search and date filters.

- Search by bill number, customer name, or phone.
- Narrow the date range if the bill is older than the default view.
- Check **Drafts** if the bill was saved but never completed.
