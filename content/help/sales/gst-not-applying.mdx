---
title: Why is GST not applying on a sale?
description: Check tax settings, item HSN, and customer GSTIN when GST is missing from a bill.
tags: [sales, gst, billing]
related:
  - /docs/sales/add-sale-retail
---

GST is calculated from the item's tax setup and the sale type. If a bill shows no GST, check these in order:

1. The drug or item has an HSN/SAC and GST rate in inventory.
2. The sale is not marked as a non-GST or estimate bill.
3. Accounting and GST settings are enabled for the store.

<Callout type="info">
  If the item tax looks correct, walk through creating the bill again from the start.
</Callout>
