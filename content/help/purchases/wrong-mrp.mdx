When you scan a purchase bill, MediFlux reads the **MRP** from the printed invoice. If the MRP looks wrong after scanning, it is almost always due to one of the three cases below.

---

## 1. The bill is not clear

If the original bill is **faded, smudged, or low quality**, the scanner can misread digits (for example, reading **90.0** as **80.0**).

- Re-scan the bill at a **higher resolution**, or use a cleaner copy.
- Make sure the MRP column is **fully visible** — not cut off, folded, or covered.
- After re-scanning, **verify the MRP** against the printed bill before saving.

## 2. The camera photo is blurry

If you uploaded a **photo of the bill taken with a camera**, poor focus, shadows, or a tilted angle can produce wrong values.

- Retake the photo in **good lighting**, with the bill **flat** and the camera **directly above** it.
- Keep the whole bill in frame and **focus on the MRP column** before capturing.
- Avoid glare and shadows, upload the clearer photo, and re-check the MRP.

## 3. The columns were mapped incorrectly

During import, every detected column must be matched to the right field. If the **MRP column is mapped to the wrong field** (or left unmapped), the values will appear incorrect.

1. In the **Map Invoice Headers** step, check the **Sample** value shown under each detected column.
2. Map the column whose sample shows the printed MRP (e.g. **90.0**) to **MRP ***.
3. Confirm the other required fields — **Drug/Product Name *** and **Quantity *** — and match **HSN Code**, **Free Quantity**, and **Units/Pack Size** to their correct columns.
4. Click **Next** and verify the values in **Review & Import** before confirming.

---

After fixing the scan, photo, or mapping, re-import the bill and **verify the MRP** in the review step. If the MRP is still wrong on a clear bill with correct mapping, contact the **MediFlux Support Team**.
