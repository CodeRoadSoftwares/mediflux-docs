You can add a purchase by uploading a photo of the supplier’s invoice. MediFlux reads the bill and pre-fills the drugs so you only need to review and finalise.

---

## Steps

1. Go to **Purchases → Add Purchase** (or use the **+** button and select **Add Purchase**).
2. Select the **supplier**, enter the **invoice number**, and set the **date** if needed.
3. Click **Scan**.
4. Upload a clear photo of the supplier invoice.
5. MediFlux scans the image and creates a draft with pre-filled drug rows.
6. Open the draft from **Drafts**, review the drugs, quantities, batch, expiry, and prices.
7. Make any corrections, then click **Add** to finalise the purchase.

## After scanning

- The scanned bill appears under **Purchases → Drafts**.
- Status can show as **Scanning**, **Completed**, or **Failed**.
- From Drafts you can **Rescan**, **Append**, **Insert**, or delete the draft.

---

<Callout type="note">
  Use a clear, well-lit photo of the full invoice for better scan accuracy. Always review the draft before finalising.
</Callout>
