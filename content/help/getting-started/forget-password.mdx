---
title: How do I invite my team?
description: Add pharmacists, cashiers, and other staff so they can sign in with their own accounts.
tags: [users, getting-started, settings]
related:
  - /docs/settings/user-management
---

Invite staff from **Settings → User Management**. Each person gets their own login so you can control what they can see and do.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Open **Settings**, then **User Management**.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Select **Add user** and enter their name, email, and role.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Save. They will receive an invite to set a password and sign in.
  </Step>
</Steps>
