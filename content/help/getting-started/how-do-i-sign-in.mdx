If you already have an **active subscription** and a store, you can sign in directly. If you do not have a store yet, create one first.

---

## Already have an active subscription?

1. Go to the MediFlux login page: [app.mediflux.in](https://app.mediflux.in).
2. Enter your registered email address and password.
3. Select **Sign in**. You will land on the dashboard for your store.

/>

---

## Do not have a store yet?

1. Go to [mediflux.in](https://mediflux.in) or the registration page and create your store.
2. Enter your pharmacy details and complete signup.
3. After your store is created (and subscription is active), sign in at [app.mediflux.in](https://app.mediflux.in) using the same email and password.

For full setup steps, see [Installation & Setup](/docs/introduction/installation) and [Getting Started](/docs/introduction/getting-started).
