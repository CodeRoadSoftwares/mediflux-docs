---
title: How do I sign in to MediFlux?
description: Steps to log in from a browser, including what to do if you forgot your password.
tags: [login, account, getting-started]
related:
  - /docs/introduction/getting-started
---

Open [app.mediflux.in](https://app.mediflux.in) and enter the email address and password for your pharmacy account.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Go to the MediFlux login page.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Enter your registered email address and password.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Select **Sign in**. You will land on the dashboard for your store.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="note">
  If you cannot remember your password, use [How do I reset my password?](/help/account/reset-password).
</Callout>
