The **Add Journal Entry** page lets you record a **manual pharmacy journal entry** in **MediFlux**. Use it for adjustments, transfers, owner drawings, expense allocations, or any other entry that is not automatically generated by sales, purchases, or payments.

Navigate here from the sidebar: **Accounting → Add Journal Entry**.

You can also reach this form by clicking **+ Add Journal** on the **Journals** page.

## Page Overview

| Element | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Transaction Date** | Date of the journal entry (required) |
| **Journal Description / Reference** | Narrative that explains the purpose of the entry (required) |
| **JOURNAL LINES** | Debit and credit rows that make up the entry |
| **+ Add Ledger Row** | Adds another debit or credit line |
| **TOTAL** | Live totals for Debit and Credit columns |
| **Cancel** | Discards the entry and returns to the previous page |
| **+ Post Journal** | Saves and posts the entry to the ledgers |

## Journal Lines

Every journal entry must follow the **double-entry rule**: total debits must equal total credits. The form enforces a minimum of two lines.

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **ACCOUNT NAME** | Select an account from the Chart of Accounts. Start typing to search. |
| **NOTE / MEMO** | Optional line-level note for extra context. |
| **DEBIT** | Amount to debit this account. Enter `0.00` if the line is a credit. |
| **CREDIT** | Amount to credit this account. Enter `0.00` if the line is a debit. |
| **Delete Icon** | Removes the line. |

### Adding More Lines

Click **+ Add Ledger Row** at the bottom of the table to insert an additional debit or credit line.

  src="https://pub-edab995818924e31a696b8a8c0fd961b.r2.dev/mediflux-docs/2026/09/fe66e0ca-79e6-48ab-b0c5-6e723bdef93a-add-jedger-row.png"

### Totals

The **TOTAL** row at the bottom of the table shows the running sum of all Debit amounts and all Credit amounts. Both totals must be equal before you can post the entry.

<Note>
The form shows the message **Min. 2 lines required (double-entry rule)** as a reminder that every journal needs at least one debit and one credit.
</Note>

---

## Posting the Entry

1. Fill in **Transaction Date** and **Journal Description / Reference**.
2. Select an account and enter either a Debit or Credit amount on the first line.
3. Select a different account and enter the balancing amount on the second line.
4. Add more lines if the entry involves more than two accounts.
5. Confirm that the Debit and Credit totals are equal.
6. Click **+ Post Journal**.

Once posted, the entry appears in:

- **Journals** with type **MANUAL** and status **POSTED**
- **Daybook** under the selected date
- The individual **Ledger** of every account used in the entry

## Example

A simple transfer of ₹5,000 from Cash to a Bank account:

| Account Name | Note / Memo | Debit | Credit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bank Account – HDFC | September transfer | `5000.00` | `0.00` |
| Cash in Hand | September transfer | `0.00` | `5000.00` |

Totals: Debit `₹5,000.00` = Credit `₹5,000.00`

## Common Mistakes

<Warning title="Debits do not equal credits">
The form will not allow you to post an unbalanced entry. Always check the **TOTAL** row before clicking **Post Journal**.
</Warning>

<Warning title="Leaving the description blank">
**Journal Description / Reference** is required. A clear narrative makes the entry easy to understand later in the Daybook and Journals list.
</Warning>
