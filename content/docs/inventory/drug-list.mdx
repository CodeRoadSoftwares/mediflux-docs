The **Drug List** is the main inventory directory in MediFlux. It gives you a real-time view of the drugs available in your store, including stock, batches, prices, expiry, suppliers, and inventory activity.

You can view inventory in three ways: **Consolidated View**, **Separated View**, and **Negative Stock**.

---

| Card | What it shows |

| **Total Drugs** | Total number of unique drugs in your inventory |
| **Total Value** | Total MRP value of your current stock, along with its cost value |
| **Expired / Out of Stock** | Number of expired drugs and drugs with no available stock |

## Inventory Views

The Drug List has three inventory views:

- **Consolidated View**
- **Separated View**
- **Negative Stock**

Each view provides a different way to work with your inventory.

---

## Consolidated View

**Consolidated View** groups matching inventory into a simpler inventory-level view.

Use it when you want to review your inventory without working with every stock entry individually.

The table includes:

| Column | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **S.No** | Row number |
| **Name** | Drug name |
| **Batch** | Batch number |
| **HSN** | HSN code |
| **Stock** | Available stock |
| **Trade P** | Trade price |
| **MRP** | Maximum retail price |
| **Expiry** | Expiry month and year |
| **Supplier** | Supplier associated with the stock |

### Selecting Drugs

Select one or more groups using the checkboxes to open the bulk action toolbar.

| Action | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Export CSV** | Exports the selected inventory data |
| **Export Activities** | Exports the activity history of the selected inventory groups |

## Warehouse Module

MediFlux can also manage stock across your **Main Store** and one or more warehouses.

**Main Store** is available by default. To add and manage warehouses, you need to subscribe to and enable the **Warehouse** module from **Settings → Modules**.

For instructions on enabling modules, see [**Modules**](/docs/settings/modules).

<Note>
The warehouse selector and stock movement options are available after the **Warehouse** module is enabled.
</Note>

### Switching Between Store And Warehouse Stock

When Warehouse Management is enabled, a warehouse selector appears with the Drug List filters.

Use it to choose which location's stock you want to view.

You can select:

- **Main Store**
- **Individual warehouse** 
- **All Warehouses**

If you have multiple warehouses, each warehouse is available separately in the selector.

<Figure
  src="/images/inventory/warehouse-filter.png"
  title="Warehouse stock filter"
  description="Switch between Main Store, individual warehouses, or All Warehouses from the Drug List."
/>

The Drug List then shows the inventory for the selected location, allowing you to work with stock from the **Main Store** or a specific warehouse.

## Moving Stock Between Locations

Stock can be moved in both directions:

- from a **warehouse to the Main Store**
- from the **Main Store to a warehouse**

Stock movement is handled from **Separated View** because you are moving quantity from a specific stock entry.

### Move Stock From A Warehouse To The Main Store

<Steps>

<Step title="Select the warehouse">

Use the warehouse selector at the top of the Drug List and select the warehouse containing the stock you want to move.

</Step>

<Step title="Open Separated View">

Select **Separated View** and find the stock entry you want to transfer.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Move">

Open the stock entry's **⋮ Actions** menu and select **Move**.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter the quantity">

The **Move Stock** window shows the drug, batch, source warehouse, current stock, and destination.

Enter the **Quantity to Move**.

You can also add **Notes** to record the reason for the transfer.

</Step>

<Step title="Move the stock">

Review the information and select **Move Stock**.

The entered quantity is moved from the warehouse to the **Main Store**.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Figure
  src="/images/inventory/move-stock-warehouse-to-store.png"
  title="Move stock from warehouse to Main Store"
  description="Enter the quantity to transfer from a warehouse to the Main Store."
/>

---

### Move Stock From The Main Store To A Warehouse

You can also move stock in the opposite direction.

<Steps>

<Step title="Select Main Store">

Use the warehouse selector and select **Main Store**.

</Step>

<Step title="Open Separated View">

Select **Separated View** and find the stock entry you want to move.

</Step>

<Step title="Select Move">

Open the stock entry's **⋮ Actions** menu and select **Move**.

</Step>

<Step title="Choose the destination warehouse">

In the **Move Stock** window, select the **Destination Warehouse**.

If you have multiple warehouses, choose the warehouse that should receive the stock.

</Step>

<Step title="Enter the quantity">

Enter the **Quantity to Move**.

The maximum quantity that can be moved is shown beside the field.

You can also add **Notes** for the transfer.

</Step>

<Step title="Move the stock">

Select **Move Stock** to transfer the quantity from the **Main Store** to the selected warehouse.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Figure
  src="/images/inventory/move-stock-store-to-warehouse.png"
  title="Move stock from Main Store to warehouse"
  description="Choose a destination warehouse and quantity when moving stock from the Main Store."
/>

<Note>
Moving stock transfers inventory between locations. It does not create new stock. The quantity is deducted from the source location and moved to the selected destination.
</Note>

## Negative Stock

The **Negative Stock** feature lets MediFlux record a sale even when the quantity being sold is greater than the available stock of a drug.

When this happens, the remaining quantity is recorded as **negative stock** instead of preventing the sale.

The **Negative Stock** tab gives you a separate view of drug entries that currently have a stock deficit and need to be reconciled. Negative quantities are shown directly in the **Stock** column.

<Figure
  src="/images/inventory/negative-stock.png"
  title="Negative Stock"
  description="View drugs with stock below zero that need to be reconciled."
/>

---

## How Negative Stock Works

Suppose you have `1` unit of **Laprox** in stock and a customer purchases `10` units.

The available `1` unit is used, but the sale still requires another `9` units.

The stock therefore becomes:

`1 - 10 = -9`

Instead of blocking the sale, MediFlux records the remaining `9` units as a stock deficit.

| Drug | Stock before sale | Quantity sold | Stock after sale |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Laprox | `1` | `10` | `-9` |

The **Laprox** entry now appears under the **Negative Stock** tab with a stock quantity of `-9`.

<Figure
  src="/images/inventory/laprox-negative-stock.png"
  title="Laprox with negative stock"
  description="Laprox appears with -9 stock after selling 10 units when only 1 unit was available."
/>

This keeps the shortage visible so it can later be corrected through **Stock Reconciliation**.

<Note>
Only stock entries with a negative quantity need to be reconciled. Reconciliation is used to clear an existing stock deficit using matching positive stock.
</Note>

---

## Reconciling Negative Stock

**Stock Reconciliation** is used when a drug already has negative stock and matching positive stock is available elsewhere in your inventory.

The positive stock may already exist under another inventory entry, including stock associated with another supplier. In that case, you can reconcile the negative stock directly.

If suitable positive stock is not currently available, stock can first be added through a purchase and then used for reconciliation.

A new purchase is therefore **not required every time you reconcile stock**.

### Reconciliation Example

After the sale above, **Laprox** has `-9` units.

Another matching **Laprox** stock entry has `22` units available. The `9` unit deficit can be reconciled using `9` units from that positive stock.

| Entry | Before | Reconciliation | After |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Negative Laprox stock | `-9` | `+9` | `0` |
| Positive Laprox stock | `22` | `-9` | `13` |

The negative entry is cleared, while the matching positive stock is reduced from `22` to `13`.

<Figure
  src="/images/inventory/laprox-reconciliation-stock.png"
  title="Positive and negative Laprox stock"
  description="Matching Laprox entries showing negative stock and positive stock available for reconciliation."
/>

---

## How To Reconcile Negative Stock

<Steps>

<Step title="Find the negative stock">

Go to **Drugs → Drug List → Negative Stock** and find the drug with the negative stock quantity you want to reconcile.

</Step>

<Step title="Open Reconcile Stock">

Go to **Separated View** and locate the negative stock entry.

Open the drug's **⋮ Actions** menu and select **Reconcile Stock**.

</Step>

<Step title="Set the reconciliation quantity">

The **Multi-Stock Reconciliation** window shows the negative drug and its current deficit.

Under **Reconcile Qty**, enter the quantity you want to reconcile. Select **MAX** to use the maximum quantity available for reconciliation.

</Step>

<Step title="Select positive stock">

Under **Positive Batches (Deducted)**, select the matching positive stock that should be used to offset the deficit.

You can search the available positive stock by **batch number, rack, expiry, or supplier**.

</Step>

<Step title="Review projected stock">

Check **Projected Stock** before confirming the reconciliation.

For example:

`-9.00 → 0.00`

means the complete `9` unit deficit will be cleared.

</Step>

<Step title="Confirm the reconciliation">

Select **Reconcile Stock**.

MediFlux adds the reconciled quantity to the negative entry to clear its deficit and deducts the same quantity from the selected positive stock.

</Step>

</Steps>

<Figure
  src="/images/inventory/multi-stock-reconciliation.png"
  title="Multi-Stock Reconciliation"
  description="Select matching positive stock and specify the quantity to use for clearing a negative stock deficit."
/>

<Note>
A purchase is not a required step in reconciliation. If matching positive stock already exists, you can reconcile directly. If no suitable positive stock is available, stock must first become available before the deficit can be reconciled.
</Note>

---

## Reconciliation Activity

Completed reconciliation is recorded in the drug's **Activity** history as **Stock Reconciliation**.

For example, if `9` units are deducted from positive **Laprox** stock containing `22` units, the activity records **Before** as `22`, **Change** as `-9`, and **After** as `13`.

`22 → -9 → 13`

This creates a traceable record of the stock deducted during reconciliation.

<Figure
  src="/images/inventory/stock-reconciliation-activity.png"
  title="Stock Reconciliation activity"
  description="Activity history showing the stock deducted from a positive entry during reconciliation."

## Add Deal

The **Add Deal** option is available from the individual drug's **⋮ Actions** menu in **Separated View**.

Use it to configure a purchase scheme for a drug, such as a buy quantity with a free quantity. You can set the **Buy Quantity**, **Free Quantity**, **Start Date**, **End Date**, and whether the deal is **Active**.

## Importing Your Inventory

If you're setting up MediFlux for the first time or need to add drugs in bulk, select **Actions → Import** to open the inventory import process.

The import process includes:

1. **Upload:** upload your inventory as a CSV or Excel file. You can also download a template to see the expected format
2. **Map Headers:** match the columns in your file to MediFlux's drug fields
3. **Preview:** review your data before confirming the import
4. **Complete:** finish the import and add the drugs to your inventory
