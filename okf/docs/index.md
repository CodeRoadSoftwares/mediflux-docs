# Documentation Sections

Progressive directory of all documentation modules in MediFlux.

* [Accounting](accounting/overview.md) - Manage pharmacy accounting in MediFlux. Track journal entries, ledgers, GST compliance, bank reconciliation, and financial reports easily.
* [Clinic Module](clinics/overview.md) - Manage your pharmacy clinic in MediFlux. Track doctor profiles, prescriptions, schedules, and patient appointments from one central hub.
* [Contributing](contributing/overview.md) - Guides and reference documentation for Contributing.
* [Customers](customers/overview.md) - Manage pharmacy customers in MediFlux. Track outstanding Udhaar payments, record sales, manage billing notes, and maintain GST-ready ledgers.
* [Introduction](introduction/overview.md) - MediFlux is India's trusted cloud-native pharmacy billing and inventory software. Streamline operations, ensure GST compliance, and grow your business today.
* [Inventory](inventory/overview.md) - Manage pharmacy drug inventory in MediFlux. Track stock levels, monitor expiries, raise debit notes, and keep your pharmacy stock accurate from one central hub.
* [Purchases](purchases/overview.md) - Manage pharmacy purchases in MediFlux. Record purchase bills, track purchase orders, restock inventory, and scan supplier invoices from one central hub.
* [Reports](reports/overview.md) - Explore MediFlux reports to monitor stock, track outstanding balances, and review GST-related business data.
* [Sales](sales/overview.md) - Manage all your pharmacy sales, credit notes, returns, reorders and drafts, all from one place.
* [Settings](settings/overview.md) - Manage your store's account details, modules, billing preferences, notifications, integrations, users, and subscriptions, all from one place.
* [Suppliers](supplier/overview.md) - Manage your pharmacy drug suppliers in MediFlux. Track outstanding payments, record purchases, and maintain a complete, GST-ready ledger from one place.
* [Store Wallet](wallet/overview.md) - Manage your MediFlux wallet balance, add credits for paid services, and track wallet transactions, deductions, and rewards.
