# Sales

* [Sales](overview.md) - Manage all your pharmacy sales, credit notes, returns, reorders and drafts, all from one place.
* [Add Credit Note](add-credit-note.md) - Issue a credit note against previously sold drugs, select the customer, choose the items, and create the note in seconds.
* [Add Retail Sale](add-sale-retail.md) - Learn how to create a retail pharmacy bill in MediFlux. Add customers, scan prescriptions, manage payments, and adjust returns quickly.
* [Add Wholesale](add-wholesale.md) - Learn how to create a wholesale pharmacy bill or Challan in MediFlux. Manage HSN codes, split taxes, adjusted returns, and bulk pricing easily.
* [Drafts](drafts.md) - Access and manage all your saved sale drafts, including bills saved mid-way and prescriptions being scanned.
* [Re-orders](re-orders.md) - Manage repeat orders and recurring sales for your regular customers.
* [Returned](returned-sales.md) - View all returned sales and process returns directly from the Sales List.
* [Sales List](sale-list.md) - View, search, filter, and manage all your recorded sales in one place.
* [Scan & Image OCR](scan-and-ocr.md) - Use your device camera to scan barcodes or capture drug labels, adding drugs to your bill instantly without manual entry.
