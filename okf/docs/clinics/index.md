# Clinics

* [Clinic Module](overview.md) - Manage your pharmacy clinic in MediFlux. Track doctor profiles, prescriptions, schedules, and patient appointments from one central hub.
* [Appointments](appointments.md) - Manage patient appointments, share a public booking link, and track appointment status and payments.
* [Doctor Dashboard](doctor-dashboard.md) - A quick look at what a doctor can see and do once their account is created, including prescriptions, schedules, and appointments.
* [Doctors](doctors.md) - View and manage doctor profiles, search by qualification or speciality, and add new doctors to your clinic.
* [Pending Bookings](pending-bookings.md) - Review and approve or reject online booking requests made through your public booking link.
* [Prescriptions](prescriptions.md) - Create prescriptions as a doctor and dispense them directly into a sale from the pharmacy side.
* [Schedules](schedules.md) - Set doctor availability, configure booking rules, and generate shareable booking links for patients.
