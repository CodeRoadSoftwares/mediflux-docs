# Getting Started

This guide walks you through setting up **MediFlux pharmacy billing software** for the first time, from creating your cloud store to making your first sale. Whether you prefer a quick self-service setup or guided onboarding with our support team, you'll be up and running within minutes.

---

## What You Need
**MediFlux** is a web-based PWA that runs on any device with a browser and internet connection. **No downloads or local servers are required.**

| Component | Minimum | Recommended |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Operating System** | Any modern OS | Windows 10+, macOS, Linux, Android, iOS |
| **Processor** | Any dual-core CPU | Intel Core i3 or above |
| **RAM** | 2 GB | 4 GB or above |
| **Internet** | Basic connectivity (2 Mbps) | Stable 4G or broadband (5–10 Mbps) |
| **Browser** | Latest Chrome, Firefox, Edge | Chrome (latest version) |

Even pharmacies in remote areas with modest connectivity and basic desktops can operate efficiently.

## Creating Your Store
You have two ways to get started with your MediFlux setup:

| Method | Best for |
| --- | --- |
| **Self-service signup** | Pharmacy owners who want to explore the platform immediately |
| **Assisted onboarding** | Teams who want help with data migration, configuration, and staff training |

### Self-Service Signup
1. Go to the [Start Free Trial](https://app.mediflux.in/registration) page and enter your pharmacy details, GSTIN, and business type.

2. Your cloud store is provisioned instantly: **no downloads, no servers, no IT team required.**

3. Log in and start exploring the MediFlux dashboard right away.

![MediFlux Pharmacy Registration Form](https://pub-edab995818924e31a696b8a8c0fd961b.r2.dev/mediflux-docs/2026/08/86e84dd3-cbb6-4f6a-93f9-c4b96d85feb3-sign-up-page.png)
*Caption: Step 1 of the MediFlux signup flow: enter your store name, address, state, district, and pincode to create your cloud account.*

### Assisted Onboarding (Remote Setup)
1. Fill out the form on the [Start Free Trial](https://app.mediflux.in/registration) page with your basic pharmacy details.

2. Our support team will contact you to schedule a guided setup process.

3. We connect remotely (via UltraViewer) to help you understand the system and give you a live walkthrough.

4. We assist with importing or manually adding your stock and customer records.

5. We guide you through billing preferences, expiry alerts, and staff role configuration.

6. Training is provided remotely so your team can start using **MediFlux** confidently from day one.

## Configuring Your Store
Once your store is created, configure these basics before you start billing:

| Setting | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **GST rates** | Set your default GST rates and HSN preferences under **Settings → Accounting Settings** |
| **Invoice templates** | Choose your bill format under **Settings → Bill Templates** |
| **Payment methods** | Configure accepted payment methods and linked bank accounts under **Settings → Accounting Settings** |
| **Customer credit policies** | Set credit limits and payment terms if you offer Udhaar (credit) sales |
| **User roles** | Add staff members and assign permissions under **Settings → User Management** |

## Adding Your Inventory
You can populate your pharmacy drug catalog in three easy ways:

| Method | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Excel import** | Upload your existing drug list via Excel. **MediFlux** pre-fills drug names, compositions, and HSN codes where available. |
| **Barcode scan** | Scan drug barcodes to auto-populate name, batch, expiry, MRP, and stock details. |
| **Manual entry** | Add medicines one by one through the **Drugs → Add Drug** form. |

## Making Your First Sale
Once your inventory is set up, you're ready to process your first pharmacy bill:

1. Go to **Sales → Add Sale** and select **Retail** or **Wholesale** mode.

2. Add a customer (or leave as a cash sale) and select drugs from your inventory.

3. Choose the payment status: **Paid**, **Unpaid**, or **Partial**.

4. Click **+ Add** to finalise the bill and print or share it via WhatsApp.

![MediFlux Add Retail Sale Screen](https://pub-edab995818924e31a696b8a8c0fd961b.r2.dev/mediflux-docs/2026/08/f5d9a796-0bca-4cef-9a58-d515f5113753-add-sale.png)
*Caption: The MediFlux retail sale form showing customer details, payment status, and the drug table ready for billing.*

Your first sale is now recorded. You can view it anytime in the [Sales List](/docs/sales/sale-list).

## FAQs

  ### How long does the free trial last?

The free trial gives you **full access for 7 days** with no credit card required.
  ### Can I switch from assisted onboarding to self-service later?

Yes. Even if you start with assisted onboarding, you retain full self-service access to all settings and features at any time.
  ### What happens to my data if I don't subscribe after the trial?

Your data remains in the system for a limited grace period. We recommend subscribing before the trial ends to avoid any interruption. Contact support if you need an extension.
  ### Do I need a GSTIN to use MediFlux?

**No.** While entering your GSTIN enables GST-compliant invoicing and reporting, you can use **MediFlux** for basic billing and inventory without it.
  ### Can I use MediFlux on multiple devices?

**Yes.** **MediFlux** syncs across all devices in real time. You can bill from your shop computer, check stock from your phone, and manage reports from a tablet.
