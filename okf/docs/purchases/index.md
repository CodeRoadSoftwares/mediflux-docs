# Purchases

* [Purchases](overview.md) - Manage pharmacy purchases in MediFlux. Record purchase bills, track purchase orders, restock inventory, and scan supplier invoices from one central hub.
* [Add Purchase](add-purchase.md) - Learn how to add a new pharmacy purchase bill in MediFlux. Manually enter drugs, scan supplier invoices via OCR, or bulk import CSV files easily.
* [AI Bill Scan](ai-bill-scan.md) - Scan pharmacy supplier invoices in MediFlux using OCR. Upload files or use your camera, map headers, and auto-import medicines into purchase bills.
* [Drafts](drafts.md) - Track the status of scanned purchase bills and clean up drafts you no longer need.
* [Purchase Details](purchase-details.md) - View the complete breakdown of a single purchase, including its summary, line items, and invoice.
* [Purchase List](purchase-list.md) - View, search, filter, and manage all your recorded purchases in one place.
* [Purchase Orders](purchase-orders.md) - Create and track purchase orders sent to suppliers before stock arrives.
* [Restocks](restocks.md) - View and manage restock orders for drugs already in your inventory.
* [Purchase Trash](trash.md) - View, restore, or permanently review purchases that have been deleted.
