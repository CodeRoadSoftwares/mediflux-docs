# Settings

* [Settings](overview.md) - Manage your store's account details, modules, billing preferences, notifications, integrations, users, and subscriptions, all from one place.
* [Account](account-info.md) - View and update your store's business details, contact information, bank details, sales settings, and store assets like logo, signature, and stamp.
* [Accounting Settings](accounting-settings.md) - Manage your accounting module's financial year status, including temporary locking.
* [Bill Templates](bill-templates.md) - Customize your invoice template, theme color, columns, tax display, and terms and conditions.
* [Data Management](data-management.md) - Permanently delete all transactional data from your store to start fresh.
* [Integrations](integrations.md) - Manage external connections and automated workflows, including WhatsApp reports and AI-powered features.
* [Invoice Counter](invoice-counter.md) - Configure invoice numbering format, counters, and auto-reset behavior for sales and purchases.
* [Modules](modules.md) - Enable or disable optional MediFlux modules and manage subscriptions for additional features like Warehouse Management.
* [Notifications](notifications.md) - Manage how you receive notifications from MediFlux.
* [Referrals](referrals.md) - Invite other store owners to MediFlux and earn rewards through the referral program.
* [Subscriptions](subscriptions.md) - View your current plan status and upgrade or renew your MediFlux subscription.
* [User Management](user-management.md) - Manage users, roles, and permissions across your platform, including sessions and activity logs.
