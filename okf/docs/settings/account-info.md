# Account

The **Account** tab holds your store's core business information, used across invoices, bills, and other documents generated by MediFlux. This is also where you manage your sales settings, store logo, signature, and stamp.

---

## Account Info

At the top, you'll see your store's name, plan status (e.g., **Pro Active**, with expiry date), and:

| Section | Fields |
| --- | --- |
| **Contact** | Phone, Email |
| **Location** | Address |
| **Bank Details** | Bank, Account Name, Account Number, IFSC |

Click **Edit** to open the **Edit Store Details** form.

## Sales Settings

The **Sales Settings** section is available at the bottom of the **Edit Store Details** form. It allows you to display profit margins during billing and receive warnings when medicines are sold at a loss.

### Available Settings

| Setting | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Show Sales Margin** | Displays a real-time profit margin column in Retail and Wholesale billing tables |
| **Warn on Negative Margin (Loss Warning)** | Shows a warning popup before saving a sale if a medicine's selling price is lower than its cost |

### Configuring Sales Settings

1. **Open Account Settings**

   Navigate to **Settings → Account**.

2. **Edit Store Details**

   Click **Edit** in the Account Info section.

3. **Find Sales Settings**

   Scroll down to the **Sales Settings** section.

4. **Choose Your Settings**

   Enable **Show Sales Margin**, **Warn on Negative Margin (Loss Warning)**, or both, depending on your requirements.

5. **Save Changes**

   Click **Save** to apply your settings.

![Sales Settings in Edit Store Details](https://pub-edab995818924e31a696b8a8c0fd961b.r2.dev/mediflux-docs/2026/10/e1a57c35-3f4f-45b0-ae8e-287f5fea3e54-show-sales-margin-accounts.png)
*Caption: Enable Show Sales Margin to display profit margins during billing, and Warn on Negative Margin to receive a warning before saving a loss-making sale.*

### Show Sales Margin in Add Sale

Once **Show Sales Margin** is enabled, a **MARGIN** column appears in the medicine table on the **Add Sale** page for both Retail and Wholesale billing.

This column displays the calculated profit margin for each medicine, allowing you to view profitability while preparing a sale.

![Sales Margin in Add Sale](https://pub-edab995818924e31a696b8a8c0fd961b.r2.dev/mediflux-docs/2026/10/96e6727c-aacd-4fea-a606-5e3e58daed06-add-sale-with-margin.png)
*Caption: The MARGIN column appears in the Add Sale medicine table when Show Sales Margin is enabled in Account Settings.*

### Warn on Negative Margin

When **Warn on Negative Margin (Loss Warning)** is enabled, MediFlux displays a warning popup if any medicine is being sold below its cost price.

The warning appears before saving the sale, helping you identify a potential loss.

> **[Note]** Both settings work independently. You can display profit margins without enabling loss warnings, enable loss warnings without displaying margins, or use both together.

## Store Assets

Below Account Info, three collapsible sections let you manage documents used on your invoices and bills:

| Asset | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Logo** | Your store's logo, shown on invoices and bills |
| **Signature** | A signature image, used on documents that require sign-off |
| **Stamp** | Your store's official stamp, used on documents that require it |

Click on any asset to expand it and view the current file. Click the asset again to open the **Change Asset** dialog, where you can drag and drop a file or browse to upload a new one (up to 2MB).

## FAQs


  ### How do I show profit margins while creating a sale?

Go to **Settings → Account → Edit**, scroll to **Sales Settings**, enable **Show Sales Margin**, and click **Save**.

    A **MARGIN** column will appear in the medicine table when creating Retail and Wholesale sales.

  ### Can MediFlux warn me if I sell a medicine at a loss?

Yes. Enable **Warn on Negative Margin (Loss Warning)** under **Sales Settings**. MediFlux will display a warning popup before saving a sale if a medicine's selling price is lower than its cost.

  ### Can I enable loss warnings without showing sales margins?

Yes. **Show Sales Margin** and **Warn on Negative Margin (Loss Warning)** are separate settings and can be enabled independently.

  ### Is there a file size limit for Logo, Signature, or Stamp uploads?

Yes, uploads are limited to 2MB per file.

  ### Can I change my store's phone number or email directly from the Edit form?

Yes, both fields have a separate **Edit** option next to them within the Edit Store Details form.
