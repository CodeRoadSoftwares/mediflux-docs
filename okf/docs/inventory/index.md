# Inventory

* [Inventory](overview.md) - Manage pharmacy drug inventory in MediFlux. Track stock levels, monitor expiries, raise debit notes, and keep your pharmacy stock accurate from one central hub.
* [Billing Notes](billing-notes.md) - Track the debit notes you've raised to suppliers and the credit notes issued back to you.
* [Catalog Share](catalog-share.md) - Generate secure, time-limited links to share your product catalog with partners and customers.
* [Create Debit Note](create-debit-note.md) - Create debit notes from the Drug List, manually, or through full and partial purchase returns, including warehouse stock.
* [Drug List](drug-list.md) - Manage your pharmacy drug inventory in MediFlux. Track batches, monitor expiries, reconcile negative stock, and import or export data easily.
* [Drug Trash](trash.md) - Learn how MediFlux handles deleted drugs, including soft delete vs hard delete behavior and how restoring or removing drugs affects your inventory and sales records.
* [Warehouse](warehouse.md) - Manage stock across your Main Store and warehouses, filter inventory by location, transfer medicines between locations, and view warehouse-wise stock details.
