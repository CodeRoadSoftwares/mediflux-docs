# Accounting

* [Accounting](overview.md) - Manage pharmacy accounting in MediFlux. Track journal entries, ledgers, GST compliance, bank reconciliation, and financial reports easily.

* [Add Journal Entry](add-journal-entry.md) - Create manual pharmacy journal entries in MediFlux. Record debits, credits, and accounting adjustments following the double-entry rule easily.
* [Balance Sheet](balance-sheet.md) - View your pharmacy balance sheet in MediFlux. Track assets, liabilities, and equity to understand your business's financial position easily.
* [Bank Accounts](bank-accounts.md) - Manage pharmacy bank accounts in MediFlux. Track balances, add accounts, and perform bank reconciliation to ensure accurate financial records.
* [Accounting Dashboard](dashboard.md) - View your pharmacy accounting dashboard in MediFlux. Track total revenue, expenses, net profit, cash flow, and financial alerts easily.
* [Daybook](daybook.md) - Track daily pharmacy financial transactions in MediFlux. View the Daybook for money received, spent, customer dues, and journal entries easily.
* [General Ledger](general-ledger.md) - View your pharmacy general ledger in MediFlux. Track detailed transaction history, running balances, and audit individual ledger accounts easily.
* [ITC Reversals](itc-reversals.md) - Manage pharmacy ITC reversals in MediFlux. Track and reverse input tax credit for expired stock, charity, and personal use to ensure GST compliance.
* [Journals](journals.md) - View and manage pharmacy journal entries in MediFlux. Search, filter, and export manual and auto-generated accounting ledgers easily.
* [Ledgers](ledger.md) - Manage your pharmacy chart of accounts in MediFlux. Track ledgers for assets, liabilities, equity, revenues, and expenses easily.
* [Profit & Loss](profitandloss-statements.md) - View revenue, expenses, and net profit for a selected period.
* [Trial Balance](trial-balance.md) - Full view with opening balance, period activity, and closing balance for all ledger accounts.
