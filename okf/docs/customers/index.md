# Customers

* [Customers](overview.md) - Manage pharmacy customers in MediFlux. Track outstanding Udhaar payments, record sales, manage billing notes, and maintain GST-ready ledgers.
* [Add a Customer](add-customer.md) - Learn how to add a new pharmacy customer in MediFlux. Create Consumer or Business profiles, track Udhaar, and manage GST-ready ledgers easily.
* [Billing Notes](billing-notes.md) - Manage debit notes raised by your connected retailers and credit notes issued to your customers, a supplier-only feature in MediFlux.
* [Customer Ledger](customer-ledger.md) - Manage your pharmacy customer ledger in MediFlux. Track Udhaar, outstanding balances, payments, refunds, and credit notes in one place.
* [Customer List](customer-list.md) - View and manage customers, track outstanding balances and credit limits, record bulk payments, filter and export customer data, and perform bulk updates or deletions.
* [Customer Profile](customer-profile.md) - View a customer's complete sales history, outstanding balance, and manage their details from one page.
* [Customer Trash](customer-trash.md) - View and restore deleted customer profiles.
* [Store Requests](store-request.md) - Accept or reject requests from other pharmacy stores on MediFlux who want to connect with you as a customer.
