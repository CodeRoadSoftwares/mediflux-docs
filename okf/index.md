# Knowledge Catalog

Welcome to the MediFlux Open Knowledge Format (OKF) documentation bundle. This catalog provides structured, link-traversable documentation for MediFlux pharmacy management software.

## Documentation Sections

* [Introduction](/docs/introduction/overview.md) - Get started with MediFlux pharmacy management software.
* [Supplier Management](/docs/supplier/overview.md) - Manage medicine suppliers, purchase ledgers, and supplier profiles.
* [Purchases](/docs/purchases/overview.md) - Record incoming inventory, purchase orders, restocks, and AI bill scans.

* [Customers](/docs/customers/overview.md) - Maintain customer profiles, ledgers, billing notes, and store requests.
* [Sales](/docs/sales/overview.md) - Process retail and wholesale sales, credit notes, and returns.
* [Reports](/docs/reports/overview.md) - View business analytics, stock reports, GST returns, and financial ledgers.

* [Clinics](/docs/clinics/overview.md) - Doctor profiles, clinic schedules, appointments, and prescriptions.
* [Settings](/docs/settings/overview.md) - Configure account details, billing templates, user permissions, and integrations.
* [Wallet](/docs/wallet/overview.md) - Track software subscription wallet balances and usage transactions.
