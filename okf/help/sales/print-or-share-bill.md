# How do I print or share a bill?

You can print or download a bill by opening its invoice from **Sales → Sales List**.

1. **Open the invoice**

   Find the sale you want to print or share. Select the three-dot menu (⋮) and choose **View Invoice**.

2. **Print or download the bill**

   In the invoice dialog, choose the required action:

   - **Print**: Print the invoice.
   - **Thermal**: Print the invoice using a thermal printer.
   - **Download**: Download the invoice to your device.
