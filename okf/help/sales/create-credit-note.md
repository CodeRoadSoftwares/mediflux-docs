# How do I create a credit note?

A **Credit Note** is used when a customer returns medicines that were previously sold. It updates the customer ledger and adds the stock back to inventory.

---

## Steps

1. Go to **Sales → Add Credit Note**.
2. Select the **date** and search for the **customer**.
3. From the list of previously sold drugs, tick the items being returned.
4. Enter the **CN Qty** (quantity to return) for each selected drug.
5. Review the summary at the bottom.
6. Click **+ Create Credit Note**.

![Add Credit Note](/images/faqs/add-credit-note.png)
*Caption: Select customer, choose sold drugs, enter CN Qty, and create the credit note.*

---

Once created, the credit note appears in the customer’s **Billing Notes** and reduces their outstanding balance.
